Privacy Policy

PAC Seating · Effective date: October 30, 2026

PAC Seating is a browser-based tool for planning and arranging seating layouts. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information stored in your browser

Seating assignments, class arrangements, calibration settings, and imported or exported planning data are handled in your browser. PAC Seating does not maintain a server-side account or database for this information.

AI Generate

If you use AI Generate, the prompt and the seating information needed to answer it are sent through the service's server-side API to Google Gemini. The Gemini API may process that request under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information in prompts.

Google Slides export

If you use Google Slides export, PAC Seating uses Google Identity Services to request your permission and uses Google APIs to create or update a presentation you select. PAC Seating does not receive or store your Google password. Google handles authentication and access tokens according to Google's policies.

Information we do not sell

PAC Seating does not sell personal information. The service does not use the seating planner to build advertising profiles.

Third-party services

The service may use Google APIs, Google Gemini, Cloudflare Pages, and GitHub Pages for hosting or functionality. Those providers may process technical information such as network requests, browser information, or API usage according to their own policies.

Data retention and deletion

PAC Seating does not intentionally retain your local seating data on its servers. You can remove locally stored browser data through your browser settings. Data sent to third-party services is handled according to those providers' retention policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.
